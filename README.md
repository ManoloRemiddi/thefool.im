# thefool.im — Major Arcana

A three-page website for browsing, studying, and reading the 22 Major Arcana
tarot cards:

- **The Cards** — visual gallery: all 22 cards as tiles; click one for the
  large image plus a short meaning and a "Key Elements" list of the image's
  symbols.
- **How to Read** — a guide to reading just the 22 Major Arcana: single
  cards, the Fool's Journey stages, spreads, upright vs. reversed.
- **AI Card Reader** — draw random cards (they land face-down and flip
  face-up) and get a streamed AI reading: one section per card plus an
  overall message. Visitors need no key and pay nothing; the AI call is
  proxied server-side and rate-limited.

Static site: plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step.
